About the platform

A clearer way to connect talent and employers

JobPortalATS brings job discovery, hiring workflows, and application tracking together in one transparent recruitment platform.

Built for a better hiring journey

Candidates can discover relevant vacancies, submit applications, and follow each stage of their progress. Recruiters can manage postings, review applicants, schedule interviews, and make informed decisions from a focused ATS workspace.

Every application follows a clear workflow from Applied through review, shortlisting, interviews, and selection, helping candidates stay informed and hiring teams stay organized.

Our focus

Transparency

Clear application stages and timely status updates.

Better collaboration

A shared workflow for candidates and hiring teams.

Responsible access

Role-based access keeps portal data in the right hands.

One platform, three experiences

For job seekers

Search jobs, maintain a professional profile, apply with your resume, and track recruiter feedback and interview progress.

For recruiters

Publish vacancies, manage your candidate pipeline, update stages, schedule interviews, and review hiring analytics.

For administrators

Oversee users, job categories, reported listings, platform activity, and messages submitted through the contact page.
